## [MS Paint and Photos secretly embed traceable GUIDs into local images](https://xusheng.dev/posts/reversing/mspaint_invisible_watermark/main/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Reverse engineering by developer Xusheng Li reveals that Microsoft Paint and Photos embed a server-issued GUID as an invisible watermark into the pixels of locally generated AI images—even when processing happens entirely on-device on Copilot+ PCs. The user&\#x27;s prompt is sent to a remote Microsoft moderation server, which returns a unique GUID that is then encoded into the image via a custom algorithm in Watermarker.dll, with no way for the user to disable it. This discovery elevates Microsoft&\#x27;s watermarking practice from an AI-content identification mechanism into a broad user-tracking and de-anonymization tool, since every image processed through these apps carries a unique traceable identifier linked to a Microsoft account. It raises serious concerns about legal subpoenas, law enforcement access, and the erosion of anonymity for anyone using these built-in Windows tools. The invisible watermark is independent of the visible watermark \(which can be toggled off\) and cannot be disabled; the same watermarkId also appears in the signed C2PA provenance metadata, creating a redundant tracking channel. The watermark is embedded into image pixels via a custom algorithm in Watermarker.dll, and Microsoft&\#x27;s server-side prompt moderation means even fully local AI generation results in network traffic to Microsoft before image saving.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Aug 24, 15:28 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421158)

**Background**: Microsoft Paint and Photos are built-in Windows applications that have recently gained AI features such as image generation and background removal, particularly on Copilot+ PCs equipped with neural processing units \(NPUs\). C2PA \(Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity\) is an open standard for embedding cryptographic provenance data into media files, and Microsoft uses it to declare AI-generated content. Invisible watermarking—steganographically embedding data into image pixels—is a technique increasingly used by AI companies to mark synthetic content, but applying it to all locally generated images with unique per-user identifiers goes far beyond content provenance.

OpenAI&\#x27;s custom Jalapeño inference chip, developed with Broadcom and Celestica, posted top results on SemiAnalysis&\#x27; InferenceX benchmark, delivering more tokens per user and more throughput per kilowatt than current state-of-the-art accelerators. OpenAI also reported 1.5–1.9× more AI work per watt, 1.7–3.6× lower end-to-end latency, and 2.1–4.1× improvements on highly interactive workloads across GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek R1 670B, and Kimi K2.5 1T. Jalapeño marks OpenAI&\#x27;s first purpose-built inference accelerator and signals a major step toward vertical integration of AI infrastructure, reducing dependence on third-party GPU suppliers. If the benchmark gains translate into production, the chip could lower latency and energy costs for ChatGPT, Codex, and API users, and intensify competitive pressure on NVIDIA and other AI accelerator vendors. Jalapeño is designed as a blank-slate LLM inference accelerator that keeps critical state local to minimize data movement and integrates networking into the architecture; it went from design to tape-out in nine months and targets a 700 W power package. The chip is intended for OpenAI&\#x27;s own infrastructure and Microsoft data center partners starting in 2026, not for direct external sale, and is meant to support both current and future LLMs across the industry.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Aug 25, 14:22

**Background**: Inference is the compute-intensive work of generating outputs from a trained model when a user sends a prompt, and unlike training, it must run continuously and interactively at massive scale. SemiAnalysis&\#x27; InferenceX \(originally launched in October 2025 as InferenceMAX\) is an open-source benchmark that continuously measures LLM inference performance across accelerators and serving frameworks, using long-context multi-turn coding scenarios to compare chips on metrics like tokens per user and throughput per kilowatt. OpenAI designed Jalapeño in partnership with Broadcom \(chip implementation and high-performance networking\) and Celestica \(board, rack, and system integration\), drawing on production insights from running GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark and other workloads.

## [Oligodendrocyte dysfunction identified as driver of age-related cognitive decline](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04608-y) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A study published in Nature Medicine on August 25, 2026 identifies oligodendrocytes as active contributors to cognitive decline in aging, leveraging a unique human brain bank with over 70 years of associated cognitive data alongside experimental modeling of oligodendrocyte dysfunction. This research reframes age-related cognitive decline by shifting focus from neurons to oligodendrocytes, opening potential new therapeutic avenues for cognitive aging and neurodegenerative diseases such as Alzheimer&\#x27;s, and highlighting the value of long-term longitudinal brain banks in uncovering mechanisms of human aging. The study&\#x27;s distinctive strength lies in its integration of postmortem human brain tissue with decades of premortem cognitive assessments and controlled experimental models, allowing researchers to correlate cellular changes with documented cognitive trajectories rather than relying solely on animal models.

rss · Nature Medicine · Aug 25, 00:00

**Background**: Oligodendrocytes are specialized glial cells in the central nervous system best known for producing myelin, the fatty insulating sheath that wraps around neuronal axons to enable rapid electrical signal transmission. Beyond myelination, they also participate in immune regulation by secreting cytokines and chemokines. Age-related cognitive decline refers to the gradual deterioration in memory, processing speed, and executive function that occurs with normal aging, and understanding its cellular and molecular underpinnings is a major goal in neuroscience and gerontology.

MetaRoCE is Meta&\#x27;s custom RDMA transport that builds on Ethernet and explicitly allows out-of-order delivery, enabling AI training and inference workloads to continue making progress despite packet loss.

As AI agents become more complex with multi-step plans, tool calls, and retries, traditional console logging fails to reveal structural bugs in agent behavior. agent-inspect addresses a growing pain point for developers building production-grade agents, where the trajectory — not just the final answer — determines correctness, and where flat logs and output-only tests miss critical failure modes. The tool runs entirely locally with no account, no collector, and no default data upload; metadata is captured by default rather than full payloads.
