Liquid Types are not complete — not every valid program can be type-checked — as the system only supports a subset of expressions with specific predicates. **Background**: Hindley-Milner \(HM\) is a classic polymorphic type inference algorithm that allows compilers to deduce types automatically without programmer annotations, famously used in ML, OCaml, and Haskell.
