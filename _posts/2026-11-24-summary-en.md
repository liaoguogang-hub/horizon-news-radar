## [Anthropic Claims VP Is Not Equal to VNP](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/tree/main/vp-vnp) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Anthropic released a formal-mathematics paper claiming that VP is not equal to VNP, separating two classes of algebraic computation problems. The paper does not solve P versus NP, but it presents a potentially significant result in the closely related arithmetic-complexity setting. A separation between VP and VNP would clarify the limitations of polynomial-size algebraic circuits and parallel computation, with possible consequences for algebraic algorithms and complexity theory. It also highlights the growing role of AI-assisted theorem proving in ambitious mathematical research. VP and VNP are the algebraic analogues of P and NP, but they concern arithmetic computation over fields rather than Boolean computation over 0 and 1 inputs. The paper explicitly states that it has not analyzed how the result relates to relativization or algebrization, important barriers in complexity theory.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 23, 22:09 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50620102)

**Background**: The P versus NP question asks whether every problem whose proposed solution can be efficiently verified can also be efficiently solved. VP versus VNP asks an analogous question for algebraic computation: whether problems solvable by nondeterministic polynomial-size algebraic circuits can also be solved deterministically with polynomial-size circuits. Such a separation would be a major complexity-theory advance, but it is not itself a solution to P versus NP.

## [FDA Approves Giredestrant for Early-Stage Breast Cancer](http://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-new-hormone-therapy-early-stage-breast-cancer-more-20-years) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The FDA approved Wilserdly \(giredestrant\) as postoperative monotherapy for adults with estrogen receptor-positive, HER2-negative early-stage breast cancer. It is the first new adjuvant endocrine therapy for this disease approved in more than 20 years. This approval introduces an additional postoperative endocrine option for the most common breast cancer subtype, potentially improving treatment choices for patients and clinicians. It represents a notable advancement in an area where existing endocrine regimens have long served as the foundation of care. Giredestrant is a selective estrogen receptor degrader: it induces an inactive estrogen receptor conformation and promotes proteasome-mediated degradation of the receptor, reducing estrogen receptor signaling that supports ER-expressing cancer cells. The approval is for use alone after surgery, and treatment selection for early-stage disease still depends on recurrence risk, patient preferences, and adverse-effect profiles.

rss · FDA Press Releases \(国际\) · Nov 23, 15:55

**Background**: Estrogen receptor-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer accounts for roughly 70% of breast cancers. Endocrine therapy is a cornerstone of systemic treatment for this subtype, with standard adjuvant courses commonly lasting about five years. Postoperative endocrine therapy can reduce long-term breast cancer mortality, but the disease is heterogeneous, so clinicians tailor treatment according to recurrence risk and tolerability.

## [NariQ Packs a 27B Model Into 10 GB](https://huggingface.co/nari-labs/Qwen3.8-27B-NariQ-GGUF) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Nari Labs released 3-bit, 2.5-bit, and 2-bit GGUF quantizations of Qwen3.8-27B using trellis-coded post-training quantization. The 3-bit file is about 10.12 GB and reportedly retains near-BF16 results across coding-agent, mathematical, reasoning, multilingual, and image-understanding evaluations. The release could make a capable 27B model practical on more consumer and workstation hardware without requiring BF16-scale storage. It also advances the shift from short-text perplexity testing toward preserving performance on long-horizon agent workloads, where repeated tool use can expose quantization errors. Reported BF16/3-bit/2.5-bit/2-bit results include DeepSWE at 39.8/41.0/41.9/28.6, Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 62.6/63.8/60.2/52.4, and AIME 25+26 at 97.7/98.3/96.5/86.0. Users currently need Nari Labs&\#x27; llama.cpp fork, and one tester reported about 3.8 tokens per second for the 3-bit file and 4.9 tokens per second for the 2-bit file on an M3 Pro, substantially slower than CUDA.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 23, 13:42 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50611402)

**Background**: Quantization reduces the number of bits used to represent model weights, lowering storage and memory requirements at the cost of potential information loss. NariQ is a trellis-coded post-training quantization method that calibrates on tokens produced by the BF16 model during agent sessions, rather than relying only on short web text and perplexity. GGUF packages weights, tokenizer data, architecture metadata, and quantization information for local inference with llama.cpp-compatible runtimes.

## [LLMs Show Latent Introspection of Injected Concepts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20031) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new paper reports that a Qwen 32B model can detect concepts introduced in earlier context and identify which concept was injected. The work frames this ability as a form of latent introspection into the model&\#x27;s own prior processing. This could improve mechanisms for detecting context tampering, unusual internal states, and potentially misleading model behavior. It also raises safety questions about whether introspective reports reliably reflect what happened inside the model rather than merely inferring it from context. The demonstrated result centers on a 32-billion-parameter Qwen model and concerns concepts injected into earlier context rather than a general claim about all large language models. The available material does not establish how robust the effect is across models, injection types, prompt formats, or whether a model can accurately conceal such injections.

rss · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 23, 22:29

**Background**: Concept injection means deliberately placing a concept, instruction, or influence into a model&\#x27;s processing environment, such as its context. Latent introspection refers to a model appearing to recognize aspects of its own internal processing, while mechanistic interpretability seeks to reverse-engineer the representations and computations behind model behavior. The finding is therefore relevant to both reliability research and AI safety, where understanding unusual internal mechanisms can matter.
