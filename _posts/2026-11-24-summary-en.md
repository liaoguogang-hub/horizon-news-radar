## [Formal Proof Links VNP to the Polynomial Hierarchy](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/tree/main/vp-vnp) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Anthropic published a machine-assisted formal-math paper establishing a relationship between the polynomial hierarchy and VNP over every field. The result advances a long-standing complexity-theory direction, but it does not itself resolve P versus NP. The claimed theorem could materially reshape lower-bound techniques in computational complexity, particularly for arithmetic and algebraic problems. It also demonstrates that AI-assisted formal proof systems can tackle research-level mathematics, while making independent scholarly validation especially important. Search results describe a change from a polynomial-order bound to a quasipolynomial bound; the same bound applies to the Hamiltonian-cycle polynomial over every field and to the permanent over every field except characteristic two. The paper also explicitly says that its relationship to relativization and algebrization has not yet been analyzed.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 23, 22:09 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50620102)

**Background**: Automated theorem proving uses computer programs to derive theorems systematically from axioms and proof rules. In computational complexity, VNP is a class associated with functions verifiable through polynomial-size computations, while the polynomial hierarchy organizes problems involving multiple alternating existential and universal quantifiers. Relating these structures may yield stronger lower bounds without proving the full P versus NP conjecture.

## [GLM-5.4 Introduces a Loop Transformer for Coding](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.4) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Z.ai released GLM-5.4, its flagship model and the first built with a Loop Transformer architecture, which reuses the same 28 layers across multiple passes. Z.ai says the design reaches intelligence comparable to models with 2–3 trillion parameters while maintaining strong coding and reasoning performance. The release challenges the usual trade-off between model capability and inference efficiency by repeatedly reusing parameters rather than moving every layer into the accelerator for each token. If its reported performance holds up in real applications, the architecture could influence how large coding and reasoning models are designed and served. GLM-5.4 combines looped parameter reuse with cached and sparse attention, per-token gating, and fused kernels intended to limit the latency and serving-cost impact of additional passes. Access is a practical caveat: the model was initially unavailable through OpenRouter or the regular Z.ai API plan, requiring users to switch to the Coding Plan for compatible API keys.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 23, 10:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50609685)

**Background**: A conventional Transformer computes each parameterized layer once while producing a token, making transfers of model weights from high-bandwidth memory to the accelerator a major inference bottleneck. A Loop Transformer reuses a fixed set of layers over multiple computational passes, allowing the model to refine its output without requiring every layer to be loaded separately each time. The approach is closely related to recurrent computation, but it retains the Transformer’s attention-based formulation.
