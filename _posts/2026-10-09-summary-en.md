## [Cloudflare Acquihires Deno, Ends Runtime Development](https://deno.com/blog/cloudflare) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Cloudflare has acquired Deno through an acquihire, committing to only one more year of runtime support with monthly bug fixes and security updates before ending development of the standalone Deno runtime. Deno will remain open source, but unless another party picks up development, it will no longer be actively maintained after that period. This effectively ends independent development of one of the most prominent Node.js alternatives in the JavaScript ecosystem, marking a significant shift in the JS runtime landscape. It also raises broader questions about the sustainability of VC-funded open-source projects and accelerates the ongoing consolidation of developer tooling into a few major platforms. The acquisition is structured as an acquihire, meaning Cloudflare is primarily acquiring Deno&\#x27;s engineering talent rather than the product itself; the team is expected to work on Cloudflare&\#x27;s Workers platform \(powered by workerd\). Deno&\#x27;s code will remain open source on GitHub, and Cloudflare has explicitly invited others to continue its development, though the project&\#x27;s roadmap and direction will shift toward Cloudflare&\#x27;s edge computing infrastructure.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Oct 9, 13:03 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50019911)

**Background**: Deno is a modern JavaScript and TypeScript runtime created by Ryan Dahl, the original creator of Node.js, with the goal of addressing Node&\#x27;s shortcomings such as security, module resolution, and built-in TypeScript support. It gained a strong following for its permission-based security model and modern developer experience, but later added npm compatibility to broaden adoption. Cloudflare is a major cloud infrastructure company whose Workers platform runs JavaScript and WebAssembly at the edge using the V8-based workerd runtime. An acquihire is a transaction where a larger company acquires a smaller one primarily to hire its talent, often when the acquired product has limited commercial viability on its own.

## [2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry Awarded for Asymmetric Synthesis](https://www.wired.com/story/life-is-asymmetric-the-scientists-who-figured-out-why-won-the-2026-nobel-prize-in-chemistry/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Henri Kagan and Kensō Soai have been awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for developing chemical reactions that produce only one mirror-image form \(enantiomer\) of a molecule, a foundational contribution to the field of asymmetric synthesis. Asymmetric synthesis is critical for the pharmaceutical industry, where different mirror-image forms of a molecule can have dramatically different biological effects—one form may be therapeutic while the other is inactive or harmful. This work underpins the precise manufacturing of safer, more effective drugs and biologically active compounds. The laureates pioneered stereoselective reactions that control chirality at the molecular level, enabling chemists to favor one enantiomer over its mirror image rather than producing a racemic mixture. Such reactions are essential tools in modern organic synthesis and have been widely adopted in industrial pharmaceutical production.

rss · Wired · Oct 8, 10:30

**Background**: Chirality refers to a property of molecules whose structures are non-superimposable mirror images of each other, much like left and right hands. A racemic mixture contains equal amounts of both enantiomers and typically does not exhibit optical activity. Asymmetric synthesis is a stereoselective reaction that creates a new chiral center while favoring one enantiomer over the other, making it indispensable for producing enantiomerically pure pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, and biologically active substances.

## [CVE-2026-94545: Next.js next/og RCE via Satori SVG Escape](https://dev.to/kozhevniko/cve-2026-94545-exploitability-deep-dive-escaping-native-parsing-and-the-non-pie-node-build-505o) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Security researchers have published a deep-dive analysis of CVE-2026-94545, a remote code execution flaw in Next.js&\#x27;s next/og image route. The exploit chains three stages: Satori failing to escape attacker-controlled text in generated SVG, native libvips/libxml2 parsing of the crafted SVG causing memory corruption, and the non-PIE official Node.js binary providing fixed addresses that remove the need for an address leak. ZoomEye shows over 1.7 million Next.js assets exposed on the internet, making any RCE in a built-in route a high-impact issue. Because the exploit requires no address leak and runs against the official, non-PIE Node binary, public PoCs become immediately portable across hosts, raising the urgency for operators to patch or apply mitigations. The vulnerable versions are Satori 0.0.27 through 0.32.x \(fixed in 0.33.5\) and Next.js 16.2.0 through 16.3.5 \(fixed in 16.3.6\); the chain only completes on the Node.js runtime with sharp installed, and the Edge runtime or setups without sharp use a sandboxed renderer that breaks the exploit. The attack is blind and crashes the worker, so attackers typically pair it with a reverse shell, making egress filtering a meaningful short-term mitigation.

rss · Dev.to · Oct 9, 22:40

**Background**: Next.js provides the next/og route through its ImageResponse helper, which uses the Satori library to convert JSX into SVG and then rasterize it into a PNG via the sharp image processing library \(which depends on native libvips and libxml2\). PIE \(Position Independent Executable\) is an ELF binary property that randomizes the base address of a program at load time, working in tandem with ASLR to make memory-corruption exploits harder; a non-PIE binary is loaded at a fixed address, allowing an attacker who already controls execution to jump to a known location without first leaking an address. CVE-2026-94545 illustrates how an escaping bug in a pure-JS layer \(Satori\) can be elevated to a native RCE when the output is fed into a C-based parser and the host runtime lacks modern binary mitigations.

On May 15, 2026, the Democratic Republic of the Congo \(DRC\) and Uganda jointly declared outbreaks of Ebola disease caused by Bundibugyo virus \(BDBV\). Within approximately 48 hours, on May 17, 2026, WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus declared the epidemic a Public Health Emergency of International Concern \(PHEIC\). By August 26, 2026, 5,815 confirmed cases and 2,788 deaths had been reported, with the vast majority \(5,794 cases and 2,786 deaths\) concentrated in the DRC across 60 health zones in six provinces. This is the ninth PHEIC declared under the International Health Regulations since 2005, triggering legally binding obligations on signatory states to respond and activating WHO-coordinated international action, including temporary non-binding travel and trade recommendations. The cross-border nature of the outbreak, combined with over 2,700 deaths and spread across six provinces, highlights persistent vulnerabilities in epidemic preparedness in Central Africa and raises global concerns about further international transmission. Bundibugyo virus \(BDBV\) is an orthoebolavirus species closely related to Zaire ebolavirus; the first known BDBV outbreak occurred in Uganda&\#x27;s Bundibugyo district in 2007–08 with a case-fatality rate \(CFR\) of approximately 36%. The current outbreak&\#x27;s CFR exceeds 47% based on reported figures \(2,788 deaths / 5,815 cases\), and transmission has reached 60 health zones, indicating extensive geographic spread within the DRC.

rss · The Lancet · 最新文章 · Oct 8, 22:30

**Background**: Ebola disease is a severe and often fatal viral hemorrhagic fever caused by orthoebolaviruses, with fruit bats suspected as the natural reservoir. A PHEIC is a formal WHO declaration under the 2005 International Health Regulations, activated when an event is serious, sudden, unusual or unexpected, crosses borders, and may require coordinated international action; it is reviewed every three months and empowers the WHO Director-General to issue temporary recommendations, including potential trade and travel restrictions.

## [Liquid AI releases open-source d1 decision models with single forward-pass inference](https://huggingface.co/blog/LiquidAI/open-d1) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Liquid AI has released d1, a family of open-weight decision models \(d1-3B and d1-omni-600M\) that process unstructured data such as text, images, or audio, read the input and one or more questions in a single forward pass, and return calibrated probabilities over a fixed set of outcomes without generating any tokens. By eliminating autoregressive token generation and producing predictions in a single pass, d1 models can deliver millisecond-latency inference on hardware ranging from NVIDIA DGX to Jetson edge devices, offering a dramatically more efficient alternative to traditional large language models for structured decision-making tasks. The d1 family includes two variants: d1-3B for text and vision tasks, and d1-omni-600M handling text, vision, and audio multimodally. Unlike LLMs that generate tokens sequentially, d1 evaluates the situation and returns probability distributions directly, making it purpose-built for structured decisions rather than open-ended generation.

rss · Hacker News \(AI/ML\) · Oct 9, 19:27

**Background**: A forward pass is a single pass of input data through a neural network from input to output, after which predictions are produced. Most large language models perform many sequential forward passes during inference because they generate output one token at a time, each new token depending on the previously generated ones \(autoregressive generation\). Decision models represent a different paradigm: instead of generating free-form text, they classify inputs into a predefined set of outcomes and return probability scores, which can often be done in a single forward pass. Liquid AI is a startup developing novel efficient AI architectures, distinct from standard transformer-based approaches.
