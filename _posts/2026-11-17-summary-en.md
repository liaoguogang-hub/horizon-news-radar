## [Jiff 1.0 Brings a Scoped Rust Datetime Library](https://burntsushi.net/jiff/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Andrew Gallant released Jiff 1.0, a Rust datetime library positioned as a modern alternative to Chrono. The release documents APIs for civil dates and times, zoned and offset datetimes, timestamps, parsing, formatting, and time-zone-aware daylight-saving-time handling. Jiff gives Rust developers another mature option for date-time work and emphasizes a “pit of success” API that makes timezone-sensitive operations harder to misuse. Its scope-first design may encourage projects to choose smaller, more focused dependencies rather than treating datetime handling as an all-purpose requirement. Jiff supports only the Unix time scale, and its crate features can configure Rust standard-library integration, Serde support, and whether the IANA Time Zone Database is embedded in the binary. The maintainer explicitly says Jiff is not appropriate for every use case; community questions also raised support for exotic scales such as TAI and UT1.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 16, 16:53 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50519744)

**Background**: Datetime libraries differ in how they represent civil dates, times, offsets, time zones, and elapsed time, while timezone rules and daylight-saving transitions often cause subtle bugs. Chrono is widely used in Rust, but Jiff was designed around a scoped API, explicit time-zone handling, and the idea that responsible dependency use includes deciding what a library should not implement.
