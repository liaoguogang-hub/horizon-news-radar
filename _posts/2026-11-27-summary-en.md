The study followed implanted arrays across a 20-year period, with individual recording durations reaching 7.3 years. The observed average decline in neural signals was modest, but the reported summary does not specify sample size, array failure rates, or the precise duration for which decoding was sustained.

**Background**: Utah arrays are silicon microelectrode arrays developed for simultaneous recording from many neurons in the brain. In intracortical BCIs, spike signals can be decoded to infer intended movements, including reaching and discrete upper-limb actions. Chronic recordings can deteriorate through increased impedance, elevated noise, lower signal amplitudes, and fewer detectable units, making long-term stability a central concern.

## [Gravity Quantized Algebraically from Null Cones](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34924) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The paper proposes a background-independent quantization of vacuum general relativity in four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime. It starts from first-order gravity, solves the gravitational constraints on intersecting null cones, promotes the reduced phase space to quantum commutation relations, and constructs an observable algebra with Gaussian wavefunctions. A successful formulation could offer a new framework for describing quantum gravity without fixing a background spacetime, aligning with a central goal of loop quantum gravity. If its axioms and path-integral construction prove mathematically consistent, the null-cone construction could provide a distinct route toward reconciling quantum mechanics with Lorentzian gravity. The construction uses the reduced phase space at finite Newton constant, deformations of the reduced action to define the algebra of observables, and Gaussian wavefunctions in a null-cone path-integral representation. Community reviewers raised unresolved questions about the resulting algebra&\#x27;s type, the rigor of the Lorentzian path integral, and whether the claimed background independence is consistent with working on intersecting null cones.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 26, 05:37 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50651863)

**Background**: General relativity treats gravity as the geometry of spacetime, while quantizing gravity requires a framework that can accommodate its dynamical and Lorentzian character. First-order gravity recasts the gravitational field in terms of tetrads and connection variables, which makes constraint solving and phase-space reduction more explicit. Loop quantum gravity is a prominent background-independent approach to quantum gravity, but the paper&\#x27;s precise distinction from existing loop-based constructions requires specialist comparison and review.
