## [Unprivileged App Can Gain Root on OnePlus 15 via OxygenOS Flaws](https://blog.nns.ee/2026/09/24/oneplus-root/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A detailed write-up reveals that two vulnerabilities in OnePlus&\#x27;s OxygenOS allow a malicious Android app requesting zero permissions to execute arbitrary code with root privileges on the OnePlus 15 \(and potentially the broader OPPO device ecosystem\). As of disclosure, the flaws remain unpatched on the latest OxygenOS build. This is significant because it bypasses Android&\#x27;s foundational permission model — a user only needs to sideload an app that asks for no permissions at all, yet the app can silently take full control of the device. It affects a current flagship handset and likely extends across the OPPO/OnePlus lineup, posing serious risk for consumers, enterprises, and anyone handling sensitive data on affected phones. The exploit chain abuses vulnerable OxygenOS system services to elevate from an unstrusted\_app domain to root without exploiting the kernel itself, distinguishing it from prior Binder-based privilege escalation bugs like CVE-2022-20421. The blog post links to lobste.rs comments, and cybersecurity outlets report the issue was disclosed responsibly but remains unpatched at the time of writing.

rss · Lobsters \(技术社区\) · Sep 29, 17:25

**Background**: Android is built on the Linux kernel and ships with root \(superuser\) access locked down by the manufacturer; normal apps run in a sandboxed unstrusted\_app SELinux domain with no privileged access. Root access grants complete control over the operating system, enabling modification of system files, extraction of sensitive data, and disabling of security features. Privilege escalation vulnerabilities on Android — whether via the Binder inter-process communication mechanism, OEM-customized services, or kernel bugs — are among the most severe classes of mobile security flaws because they can turn an ordinary app into a fully privileged attacker.

## [Phase 1 trial of WRN inhibitor shows synthetic lethality in MSI cancers](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04693-z) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A Phase 1 dose-escalation trial of RO7589831, a first-in-class inhibitor of Werner syndrome helicase \(WRN\), demonstrated safety and partial responses in patients with microsatellite instability \(MSI\) and/or DNA mismatch repair-deficient solid tumors, with the maximum tolerated dose not reached. This trial establishes WRN inhibition as a viable synthetic lethal strategy for MSI cancers, offering a targeted treatment alternative for patients who do not respond to or cannot tolerate immune checkpoint inhibitors—the current standard of care for this tumor type. RO7589831 is described as a first-in-class WRN helicase inhibitor. The trial is still in early-phase dose escalation, and the partial responses observed—while promising—represent initial efficacy signals rather than confirmed clinical benefit.

rss · Nature Medicine · Sep 29, 00:00

**Background**: Synthetic lethality is a therapeutic strategy in which a drug targets a vulnerability that cancer cells uniquely depend on due to a specific genetic alteration, sparing normal cells. Microsatellite instability \(MSI\) arises when DNA mismatch repair \(MMR\) machinery—comprising genes such as MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, and PMS2—is defective, causing widespread mutations in repetitive DNA sequences. MSI-high tumors are currently treated primarily with immune checkpoint inhibitors, but many patients fail to respond. WRN helicase is an enzyme involved in DNA repair and replication, and MSI cancer cells become uniquely dependent on WRN for survival, creating a synthetic lethal vulnerability.

## [Oral GLP-1 Agonist Safiglipron Cuts HbA1c in Phase 3 Trial](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04651-9) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OUTSTAND-1三期试验表明，每日一次口服小分子GLP-1受体激动剂safiglipron在32周内使早期2型糖尿病（未使用降糖药物）患者的HbA1c较安慰剂降低最多1.45%，但体重减轻效果较为温和。该结果于2026年9月29日发表于《Nature Medicine》。 As an oral small-molecule GLP-1 agonist, safiglipron could eliminate the need for injectable administration that has long been a barrier to GLP-1 therapy adoption, potentially improving patient adherence and accessibility. The strong HbA1c reduction positions it as a viable early-treatment option, though the modest weight loss differentiates it from injectable counterparts like semaglutide. Safiglipron is a nonpeptidic agent that binds primarily through non-canonical orthosteric sites within the GLP-1 receptor transmembrane domain, enabling selective G protein \(Gs\)-biased signaling with reduced β-arrestin-mediated adverse effects. The trial enrolled participants with type 2 diabetes who were not on glucose-lowering medication, and the 1.45% HbA1c reduction is clinically meaningful against placebo.

rss · Nature Medicine · Sep 29, 00:00

**Background**: GLP-1 receptor agonists are a class of drugs that mimic the action of glucagon-like peptide-1, a hormone that stimulates insulin secretion in response to food intake. They enhance glucose-stimulated insulin release, suppress glucagon output, slow gastric emptying, and promote satiety, making them effective for both blood sugar control and weight management. HbA1c \(hemoglobin A1c\) is a blood test that reflects average blood sugar levels over approximately three months and is the standard metric for diagnosing and monitoring diabetes. Traditional GLP-1 agonists such as semaglutide and liraglutide are peptide-based and require subcutaneous injection, which has limited uptake—oral small-molecule alternatives like safiglipron aim to overcome this barrier.
