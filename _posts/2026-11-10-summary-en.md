## [ANGPTL4 Inhibition Lowers Triglycerides in Phase 2b Trial](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2612463?ai=nejm&amp;af=R&amp;rss=currentIssue) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The phase 2b TYDAL-TIMI 78 trial found that the ANGPTL4 inhibitor MAR001 significantly reduced fasting triglyceride levels after 12 weeks in adults with elevated triglycerides. The study was presented at AHA 2026 and published simultaneously in the New England Journal of Medicine. ANGPTL4 inhibition could provide a new treatment option for hypertrlyceridemia by enhancing lipoprotein lipase activity and improving triglyceride clearance. Its potential cardiovascular relevance will depend on whether the lipid changes translate into better clinical outcomes, which requires further studies. MAR001 is an investigational monoclonal antibody funded and developed by Marea Therapeutics, and the two-part, double-blind phase 2b trial was registered as NCT07028749. The reported efficacy finding concerned fasting triglycerides at week 12; the available summaries do not provide enough information to assess adverse events, dose-specific effects, or longer-term outcomes.

rss · NEJM · 最新文章 · Nov 9, 02:00

**Background**: ANGPTL4 is an angiopoietin-like protein that can inhibit lipoprotein lipase, an enzyme involved in breaking down triglycerides in circulating lipoprotein particles. ANGPTL4 inhibitors are intended to increase lipoprotein lipase activity and thereby lower triglycerides. Elevated triglycerides are a recognized lipid abnormality, but reducing them does not by itself establish a reduction in cardiovascular events.

## [Survodutide Meets Cardiovascular Safety Endpoint in Obesity Trial](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736%2826%2902140-9/fulltext?rss=yes) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The phase 3 SYNCHRONIZE-CVOT trial found that survodutide did not increase major adverse cardiovascular events compared with placebo in adults with obesity who had established cardiovascular disease, chronic kidney disease, or elevated cardiovascular risk. The trial therefore met its primary cardiovascular safety endpoint. This provides cardiovascular-safety reassurance for survodutide, a novel dual agonist targeting both GLP-1 and glucagon receptors that is being developed for obesity treatment. The result supports continued evaluation of the drug in a population for whom cardiovascular complications are especially important. The primary composite endpoint included cardiovascular death, non-fatal stroke, non-fatal myocardial infarction, ischemia-related coronary revascularisation, and heart-failure events. The study was a global, randomised, double-blind, parallel-group, placebo-controlled, multicentre, event-driven phase 3 trial, and the supplied report focuses on cardiovascular safety rather than demonstrating superiority over placebo.

rss · The Lancet · 最新文章 · Nov 9, 14:45

**Background**: Survodutide is an investigational peptide developed by Boehringer Ingelheim through an agreement with Zealand Pharma that activates both glucagon and GLP-1 receptors. Cardiovascular-outcomes trials assess whether a therapy causes more serious cardiovascular events than placebo, which is particularly important when prescribing new drugs to people with obesity and existing disease. Although major adverse cardiovascular event definitions can vary among studies, SYNCHRONIZE-CVOT used a five-part composite endpoint covering cardiovascular death, ischemic events, revascularisation, and heart failure.

## [Deep-Learning Gait Model Predicts Multisystem Health](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04725-8) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers developed a gait foundation model using three-dimensional skeletal motion recorded from 3,328 deeply phenotyped adults during five motor tasks. Its embeddings significantly predicted 1,887 of 3,210 phenotypic targets and improved predictions of clinical diagnoses and medication use. The model suggests that ordinary movement captured with a depth camera can serve as a broad digital biomarker for health beyond movement disorders. It could support earlier assessment, diagnosis, and medication-related decision-making across many organ systems, although further validation is needed before clinical use. The model was trained on skeletal-motion data collected with a depth camera during five motor tasks. It showed predictive gains across all 18 body systems in men and 17 of 18 in women, while also improving prediction of clinical diagnoses and medication use.

rss · Nature Medicine · Nov 9, 00:00

**Background**: A gait foundation model learns reusable representations from locomotion data that can be transferred to multiple downstream prediction tasks. Here, the learned embeddings connect three-dimensional skeletal movement with clinical and physiological traits, forming the basis of a digital biomarker. Depth cameras can provide structured movement measurements without requiring motion-capture suits or body-mounted sensors.

Coding agents often recognize modern web features but still default to JavaScript-heavy patterns from around 2019. The package offers search and legacy-scan commands, with the scanner trained on 30,000 GitHub repositories and reporting 92% average precision on held-out repositories. ## [AlphaProof Code Brings AI to Machine-Level Verification](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, a multi-agent AI framework that writes formal specifications and machine-checked proofs in Lean and Rocq. The team used it to verify key security properties of libsignal, Signal’s reference protocol implementation, down to machine instructions. Automating the creation of large proof artifacts could make formal verification more practical for security-critical software, where conventional approaches are often costly and labor-intensive. The work may help teams establish stronger guarantees for cryptographic and other high-impact systems, although it does not remove the need for expert review. AlphaProof Code operates through multiple agents that iterate over a codebase, write Lean or Rocq proofs, and run the proof tools for feedback. Search results report more than three million lines of machine-checked proof written in Lean, while another result characterizes the effort as 6.5 million lines of checked proof produced in four weeks; the figures appear to describe related measurements and should not be treated as interchangeable.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 10, 12:11 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50439193)

**Background**: Formal verification checks that software satisfies a formal specification by producing mathematical proofs that can be mechanically validated. Lean and Rocq are proof assistants commonly used to express and check such properties, with Lean also influential in mathematics, hardware, software verification, and AI. Verifying an implementation against a specification does not establish that the specification itself captures every real-world requirement, a distinction central to reactions to the project.

## [What’s New in the .NET 11 Runtime](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/core/whats-new/dotnet-11/runtime) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The .NET 11 runtime introduces runtime-managed async support, async tiering, and tail-await optimizations, along with updated ReadyToRun compilation targets and modern hardware requirements. The release also includes compatibility-sensitive behavioral changes that application teams should review before upgrading. These runtime changes can improve async application performance and reduce reliance on compiler-generated state machines, while modernized native code targets may benefit contemporary processors. Because .NET 11 raises minimum hardware requirements and changes some runtime behavior, enterprise and cross-platform developers should assess deployment and compatibility implications. ReadyToRun targets now take advantage of newer hardware capabilities, and the minimum instruction-set requirements have increased for x86, x64, and Arm64.
