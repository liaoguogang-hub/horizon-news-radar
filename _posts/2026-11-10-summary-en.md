## [AlphaProof Code Extends AI to Software Verification](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Google DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, an AI proof agent that generates formal proofs for software implementations, with every proof checked by Lean. The work includes formalizing codebases and verifying properties of implementation code against written specifications. The project could make formal verification more scalable for complex software by reducing the manual burden of writing proofs. This may improve assurance in security-sensitive code and could eventually change how critical software is developed, tested, and maintained. Lean serves as the machine-checkable proof environment, ensuring that accepted proofs are independently verified rather than relying solely on the AI&\#x27;s judgment. A central limitation is that formal verification establishes only the properties encoded in the specification; it does not automatically prove that the specification captures every intended behavior or that the system is bug-free.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 10, 12:11 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50439193)

**Background**: Formal verification uses mathematical reasoning and automated tools to demonstrate that software satisfies a formal specification. Historically, this approach has required substantial time and specialist expertise, which has limited its use in ordinary development. AI proof systems such as AlphaProof Code seek to automate much of that labor while retaining the rigor of a theorem prover such as Lean.

## [FDA Approves First Factor XIa Inhibitor for Stroke Prevention](http://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-factor-xla-inhibitor-help-prevent-stroke-certain-adults-high-risk) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The FDA approved Sunharbo \(asundexian\) 50 mg tablets, the first factor XIa inhibitor, for use with antiplatelet therapy to reduce stroke risk after a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke or high-risk transient ischemic attack. The approval introduces a new oral anticoagulant option for selected adults at elevated risk of recurrent stroke. This is a major regulatory milestone because factor XIa represents a new anticoagulant target and could expand stroke-prevention treatment beyond established approaches. It may benefit carefully selected patients while offering a potentially different balance between clot prevention and bleeding risk. Asundexian is approved at a 50 mg tablet dose and is intended for combination use with antiplatelet therapy, rather than as a stand-alone treatment. The indication is limited to adults following a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke or high-risk transient ischemic attack, so it does not apply to all stroke types or patients with other forms of atrial embolism.

rss · FDA Press Releases \(国际\) · Nov 9, 22:15

**Background**: Factor XIa is part of the intrinsic coagulation pathway and contributes to thrombin generation and fibrin formation, making it an attractive target for anticoagulants intended to reduce clot-related stroke. Antiplatelet therapy and anticoagulants act through different mechanisms and are often combined after certain acute ischemic events. Non-cardioembolic strokes are those not caused by a clot originating in the heart, while a transient ischemic attack produces temporary neurological symptoms without lasting brain infarction.

## [Divarasib Extends Survival in KRAS G12C Lung Cancer](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41591-026-00057-9) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Phase 3 trial data published by Nature Medicine show that the next-generation KRAS G12C inhibitor divarasib prolonged progression-free survival compared with the first-generation inhibitors sotorasib and adagrasib in patients with non-small-cell lung cancer. The comparison involved 338 patients, with 167 assigned to divarasib and 171 assigned to control treatment. Divarasib could become a preferred option for previously treated patients whose tumors harbor the KRAS G12C mutation, challenging the current standard offered by sotorasib and adagrasib. The result also reflects broader progress in overcoming resistance to KRAS-targeted therapies. The trial&\#x27;s median follow-up was 14.3 months; 156 control-group patients received sotorasib and 15 received adagrasib. Divarasib, also known as GDC-6036, is an oral, covalent KRAS G12C inhibitor designed for high potency and selectivity, although the supplied report does not specify the magnitude of the survival benefit or safety outcomes.

rss · Nature Medicine · Nov 10, 00:00

**Background**: KRAS G12C is a specific mutation in the KRAS oncogene and is found in a subset of non-small-cell lung cancers. KRAS G12C inhibitors bind covalently to the mutant protein to block its signaling activity. Although sotorasib and adagrasib established the first generation of this targeted therapy, intrinsic and acquired resistance can limit their effectiveness, motivating trials of next-generation agents such as divarasib.

Resistance exercise loads muscle and is commonly used to preserve muscle during weight-reduction programs. Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry, or DXA, is a standard imaging method for quantifying lean and fat mass and assessing body-composition changes.

## [Modern Web Guidance Teaches Coding Agents Native Browser Primitives](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/modern-web-guidance) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and MDN collaborators released the open-source Modern Web Guidance 1.0 skill set, containing more than 200 implementation guides for modern CSS and browser APIs. Its search-based CLI, installer, and legacy-pattern scanner help coding agents select platform-native features and identify older JavaScript-heavy patterns. Coding agents often know modern browser features by name but still default to legacy div-and-JavaScript implementations. By encoding current platform practices as reusable agent guidance, the project aims to produce simpler, more accessible, and more maintainable web interfaces while improving the consistency of generated code. The roughly 1.7 MB corpus is distributed as a pure-JavaScript package reduced from an initial 500 MB native stack, with search taking about 10 milliseconds on the user&\#x27;s CPU. A deterministic legacy-scan model trained on 30,000 GitHub repositories reported 92% average precision on held-out repositories, while evaluations across 200 tasks and about 1,700 Playwright- and AST-based assertions improved agent pass rates from 55% without guidance to 92% with it; these are project-reported results rather than independent validation.

**Background**: The CSS Custom Highlight API can style arbitrary text ranges without scattering wrapper elements throughout the document.
