The multicentre, single-arm, phase 2 NICHE-2 trial reported 100% three-year disease-free survival and 100% five-year overall survival in patients with locally advanced mismatch repair-deficient colon cancer treated with neoadjuvant ipilimumab and nivolumab before surgery. rss · The Lancet · 最新文章 · Oct 21, 22:30

**Background**: Mismatch repair deficiency is associated with microsatellite instability, which can make colorectal cancers more immunogenic and particularly responsive to immune checkpoint inhibitors. Neoadjuvant therapy is given before surgery with the aim of shrinking or eliminating tumour and testing biological response, while checkpoint inhibitors restore T-cell activity through pathways such as CTLA-4 and PD-1. NICHE-2 evaluates whether this approach can produce durable outcomes in locally advanced dMMR colon cancer.

The biomarker was measured in the pre-infusion CAR T product rather than relying only on patient characteristics before treatment. CCR7 and CD45RA help distinguish T-cell states, including naive-like and T-memory stem cell-like populations that can retain greater proliferative capacity and persistence than more differentiated effector T cells. Prior studies linked CD8+CD45RA+CCR7+ cells with CAR T expansion, while newer work has investigated manufacturing CAR T products from preselected naive or stem-memory cells.
