## [Personalized Vaccine Boosts Melanoma Recurrence-Free Survival](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2609762?ai=nejm&amp;af=R&amp;rss=currentIssue) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial found that adding intratumoral intismeran autogene to adjuvant pembrolizumab improved recurrence-free survival in patients with completely resected stage IIB to IV melanoma. At two years, recurrence-free survival was 82.2% with the combination, compared with 70.0% with pembrolizumab alone. This result could establish a new postoperative treatment option for patients at substantial risk of melanoma recurrence. It also expands the use of individualized neoantigen-based mRNA therapy beyond earlier-stage melanoma populations studied in phase 2b. Intismeran autogene is an individualized synthetic mRNA therapy that encodes up to 34 patient-specific neoantigens identified from the resected tumor through genomic sequencing and a proprietary algorithm. The reported finding centers on recurrence-free survival; the search results also describe an exploratory overall-survival analysis at five years, but its confidence interval includes 1, so that signal requires cautious interpretation.

rss · NEJM · 最新文章 · Nov 11, 10:00

**Background**: Adjuvant pembrolizumab is an established systemic immunotherapy option for many patients with resected stage IIB or higher melanoma, where the goal is to reduce recurrence after surgery. Neoantigens are tumor-specific abnormal proteins that can be targeted by a vaccine to help the immune system recognize and attack the cancer. Intismeran autogene is administered intratumorally and is designed to encode a personalized set of these targets, complementing pembrolizumab&\#x27;s checkpoint blockade.

## [Rust 1.100 Stabilizes Never Types and Custom Allocators](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/11/12/Rust-1.100.0/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Rust 1.100.0 stabilizes never types and custom allocators, while Cargo adds dependency publish-age cooldowns and default warnings for unused dependencies. The release also introduces numerous API and tooling improvements, including DropGuard, bool::toggle\(\), Box::map\(\), string and path trimming methods, and lockfile-aware cargo-install behavior. These changes improve Rust&\#x27;s type-system expressiveness, allocation flexibility, dependency hygiene, and supply-chain resilience. They may affect library authors, Cargo users, and organizations balancing the risk of newly published packages against the operational friction caused by strict cooldown policies. When configured, Cargo&\#x27;s minimum-publish-age policy excludes versions published too recently, unless they are already recorded in Cargo.lock, and depends on registries exposing publication timestamps. Cargo now emits an unused dependency warning by default, while cargo-install reuses an existing lockfile from the .crate archive and treats --locked as a no-op.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 12, 18:39 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50472038)

**Background**: The never type represents values that cannot return normally, making it useful for expressing diverging control flow and unreachable branches. Custom allocators let Rust programs use specialized allocation strategies for particular data or tasks rather than relying exclusively on the global allocator. Dependency cooldowns are intended to give newly published packages time to be observed before automated tools adopt them, but they are not foolproof and can require exceptions.
