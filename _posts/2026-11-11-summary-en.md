## [Can Linear Programming Edit Neural Network Weights?](https://nathan.rs/posts/weight-space-lp/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The article investigates whether linear programming can formulate constrained neural-network weight edits as a weight-space programming problem. It explores using explicit behavioral constraints to find acceptable weight modifications without relying on a conventional gradient-based training loop. If effective, this approach could offer a principled alternative to reinforcement learning for edits that must improve one behavior while strictly preserving others. It may also broaden the operational capabilities of weight-editing systems that directly modify model parameters. The central mechanism is to express desired and forbidden behaviors as linear constraints, then solve for a suitable set of weights. The discussion identifies practical caveats: the quality of the linear formulation matters, solver behavior can influence the result, and a linear program is not automatically capable of carrying out an open-ended instruction such as improving a model&\#x27;s mathematics.

hackernews · Hacker News \(热门\) · Nov 11, 12:30 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50452655)

**Background**: Linear programming optimizes variables subject to linear equality and inequality constraints, traditionally producing a global optimum when feasible and bounded. Weight-space programming instead means directly reading and editing another neural network&\#x27;s parameters to satisfy a goal, without a standard gradient-descent training loop. Knowledge distillation is related because it transfers behavior or knowledge from one model to another, and constrained weight edits could potentially make that process more surgical.
